About

What is Astrophysics?

Astrophysics is the branch of astronomy that applies the principles of physics and chemistry to understand the universe — stars, planets, galaxies, and the cosmos itself. It focuses on the "how" and "why" behind what we observe, using physical laws to explain phenomena like star formation, black holes, and the origins of the universe.

Modern astrophysics grapples with major mysteries: dark matter, dark energy, stellar explosions, galaxy formation, and the potential for life on exoplanets.

Important Concepts

Planetary Science

Exploring how planets, moons, asteroids and comets form, evolve and shape our solar system.

Deep Space

Studying galaxies, nebulae, black holes, and the large-scale structure of the cosmos.

Star Systems

Uncovering how stars and their planetary systems form, evolve, and interact across the galaxy.
